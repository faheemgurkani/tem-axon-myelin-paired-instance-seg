# Chuter … Jablonski 2026: optic-nerve axon tools fail independent validation

**Citation:** Chuter B, Emmert N, … Jablonski MM. "Comparison of Deep Learning Tools for Optic Nerve Axon Quantification Finds Limited Generalizability Upon Independent Validation." *Bioengineering* 2026, 13(6):647. doi:10.3390/bioengineering13060647. Published 30 May 2026 (**peer-reviewed**).
**Local copy:** `docs/references/papers/chuter2026_bioengineering_optic_nerve_dl_tools_validation.pdf` (+ `chuter2026.txt`). Read in full, 15 Nov 2026.

**Correction to our earlier notes:** the 13 Nov report described it as "bioRxiv, Aug 2026 (preprint), benchmark of 18 nerve-morphometry tools". The published version is a PRISMA-ScR scoping review (2,036 records → 4 manuscripts → 3 models) plus independent validation of those 3 models (AxoNet, AxoNet 2.0, AxonDeepSeg). Cite the journal version.

**What they did:**
- **Modality:** light microscopy (PPD / toluidine blue), *not* EM.
- **Rat set:** 44 images, 6,941 manually annotated axons, 256×256 tiles at 0.055 µm/px.
- **Mouse set:** 74 whole cross-sections; ground truth is stereological counts, with no per-tile masks.
- **Metrics:** count agreement (Pearson, CCC, MAE, bias, Bland–Altman) and pixel metrics (Dice, IoU, precision, recall).

**Findings:**
- **Published agreement doesn't transfer.** Published r = 0.959–0.99. Independent: rat r = 0.83–0.91, mouse r = 0.57–0.74.
- **Rat pixel metrics:** precision > 0.94, recall 0.18–0.27, Dice 0.29–0.40. The models are conservative and undercount by ~70% (bias −113.7 axons/tile).
- **Correlation hides it:** "strong rank-order agreement masks systematic undercounting".
- **Model rankings flip** between the rat and mouse datasets, and between published and independent results.
- **Limitations:** the mouse ground truth has no per-tile masks; different staining and scale.

**Use for us:**
- **C1 support:** aggregate or correlation metrics hide per-object error structure. That argues for a per-object error decomposition, not only a summary score. It is a different modality, so cite it as cross-domain support.
- **H4 (rankings are dataset-dependent) is supported.**
- Report recall next to agreement metrics (D-08 already does this).
